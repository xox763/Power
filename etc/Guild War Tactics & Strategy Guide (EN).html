Guild War Strategy & Attack Guide

A simple guide on how we plan our attacks, adapt to enemy defenses, and work together to win Guild Wars.

1. Custom Game Plans Based on Past Wars

Instead of guessing every war, we use our past battle records to build a custom attack plan for each enemy guild.

  • Before the war: We check the enemy guild's history and lineups to decide the best path, main targets, and team matchups.
  • Right when the war starts: If the enemy changes their defense lineup, we spot it right away, adjust our plan, and share the new targets with everyone.
Why this matters: Catching a defense swap early saves attacks and keeps everyone hitting the right targets.

2. Our Three Main Attack Plans

Depending on how strong the enemy defense is, we follow one of three game plans:

Plan A: When the Bridge is too strong to break (Rare right now)

  • Focus on taking Mage Academy, Barracks, Lighthouse, and Bridge first.
  • Once those are down, check if any other building can be taken and hit it.
  • If no other buildings can be cleared, each player picks one weaker enemy team and attacks it.
  • Don't force rematches: If you lose an attack, don't keep hitting the same team just to get a win. Hit a fresh target that hasn't been attacked yet to earn as many points as possible.

Plan B: When we can break the Bridge, but the Citadel is too tough

  • After clearing the Bridge, we list all the other buildings we can realistically capture (skipping the Citadel).
  • Taking as many buildings as possible gives us the highest total score.
  • Leaders will share which buildings have top priority.
  • Early & Mid War: You don't have to follow a strict order yet—just pick an enemy you can beat from the target list.
  • Late War: Priority order becomes strict. Leaders will assign specific targets so we finish every open building cleanly.

Plan C: When we can capture the Citadel

  • Leaders will post the shortest path to the Citadel: Bridge → One of the three Gates → Citadel.
  • Stay on the path: Until the Citadel falls, do not waste attacks on buildings outside the main path.
  • Backup targets: If you can't log in at the right time or something unexpected comes up, leaders will assign you a target in SoE (Spring of Elements) or Foundry so your attacks aren't wasted.
  • After the Citadel falls: Leaders will post the priority order for clearing the remaining buildings.

3. Bridge Strike Team

  • Team size: 3 to 4 players.
  • Goal: Break through the Bridge within the first hour of the war.
  • Enemy lineups: Bridge defenses are usually Earth + Light or Dark + Light teams.
  • Who fits best: Players with a strong Fire team who can log in early when the war begins.
  • Extra attacks: If you still have attacks left after the Bridge drops, move right onto the next Gate.
Everyone can help: Even though we have a dedicated Bridge group, don't wait around if you're online. Anyone who has the right team to beat a Bridge defender should jump in and attack!
