Titan & Totem Strategy Guide

Manual Battle Control, Totem Timing & Defense Optimization

1. Manual Attack Basics

  • When to Use: Highly recommended when facing equal/stronger teams or heavily counter-based matchups.
  • Practice Grounds: Use Guild Dungeon or TotE (Tournament of Elements) to get comfortable with skill timing.

2. Element-Specific Totem Tactics

  • Light Totem vs. Light Totem: Activating first is usually a disadvantage. Against a similar-power team, wait until the enemy triggers theirs before popping yours.
  • Earth Totem: Generally best to activate immediately. However, against a strong opponent, time your Barrier to block their high-damage skill bursts.
  • Water Totem or The Depth Whisper Skill: Best used for sustain. Tank massive incoming damage using Light Totem invulnerability first, then heal up with Water Totem.
  • Earth Totem Barrier Counter: To avoid taking damage reflection, it is best to destroy the enemy Earth Barrier using a Totem skill. If Brustar is alive, his Wormhole skill can also chip down the barrier without triggering reflection damage.

3. Dual Totem & Skill Allocation

  • In Dual Totem setups, one totem almost always triggers first.
  • Carefully assign active vs. passive Totem Fusion skills to maximize utility based on activation order.

4. Auto Mode & Defense Setup

  • Test team lineups and skill trigger timings beforehand to build a solid defense setup.
  • Since Titan compositions are limited, copying top-ranking teams from ToT or TotE is a very effective shortcut.

5. Nuke Strategy & Tradeoffs

  • Nuke Strategy: If facing an overwhelming enemy where you can only cast once, accumulate maximum Totem Skill Power to launch a single high-damage burst.
  • Tradeoff Warning: Holding skills too long can cost you the match—finding the optimal timing requires hands-on practice.
💡 Key Takeaway: Practice skill timing across different elemental matchups to instinctively know when to hold or burst!
