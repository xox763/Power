Hero Wars: War Flags Guide

Comprehensive overview and evaluation of each War Flag

"Based on my experience, here is a general breakdown and evaluation of each War Flag..."
Pet Strength All-Around

A solid, all-around flag that works with almost any team composition. It's usually the go-to safe choice whenever you're unsure what to pick.

Fervor Low Popularity

Not very popular. It's useful when your tank needs to cast their Ultimate faster. It might be worth considering in mirror matchups, though it still requires testing and validation to see if you actually gain a timing advantage.

Recovery Defensive

A defensive flag best used in teams built around strong healers, such as Guus, Martha, Thea, and Celeste.

Swift Warriors Offensive

An offensive flag specifically tailored for Warrior-meta teams. It has limited use cases, mainly with heroes like Ishmael, Arachne, Lyria, or Qing Mao.

Frost Long Battle / Counter Control

Designed for long, drawn-out battles. Shines especially well against control-heavy teams. (Note: A skill level dropped by 2 reduces its success rate to 66%).

Readiness Backline Focus

Helps you gain an advantage by speeding up the Ultimate timing of your backline hero. Depending on who is in your backline, it can be played either offensively or defensively. Works great with Aidan or Lara Croft.

Vanguard Long Battle / Frontline

Built for long battles. Great if you want to extend the fight by boosting the survivability of your frontline heroes, such as Electra, Galahad, Julius, etc.

Bastion Defensive / Physical Armor

A defensive flag. Works very well when paired with a sturdy tank (maybe except for Electra?) when facing physical damage burst teams.

Decline Low Popularity

Not very popular overall.

Steel Storm Multi-Target

Highly effective when paired with multi-target damage dealers like Dante, Keira, Isaac, or Lara Croft. Also, keep in mind that all tanks inherently hit multiple targets on the same line.

False Immortality Long Battle / Dorian Alt

Designed for long battles—essentially acts as a War Flag alternative to Dorian. While Dorian has a limited aura range with uncapped healing, this flag provides a team-wide aura with capped healing, making it great for teams with sustained damage dealing.
